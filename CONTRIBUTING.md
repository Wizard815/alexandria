Thanks for helping. Bug reports, ideas, fixes and mods are all welcome.

a paid hosted service or give others permission to. So that your work can be
part of every version, each pull request needs this agreement.

5. Your contribution comes as is, with no warranty.

Tick the agreement box in the pull request template to confirm.

## Mods

A mod you share through `mods/` must follow the free-mods policy in
[docs/LICENSING.md](docs/LICENSING.md#mods): free to download, donation links
are fine, no paywalls.
