# Licensing in plain English

Alexandria's code is under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](../LICENSE).
This page explains what that means for you. It is a summary, not legal advice,
and the license text is what applies if the two ever disagree.

Shield is a *source-available* license. The code is public and free, but it is
not "open source" in the official OSI sense, because it limits one kind of use:
competing with Alexandria.

## What you can do, free

- Read, download, build and run Alexandria.
- Run it for your own table, your friends, your club or your Discord, online or
  in person.
- Use it while you DM, including games where players pay you to run them.
- Change it, fix it and share your changes back with a pull request.
- Write mods, templates and worlds for it, and share them.
- Use pieces of it inside your own project, as long as that project doesn't
  compete with Alexandria.

## What needs permission

- Selling Alexandria, or a copy or fork of it.
- Offering Alexandria, or a fork of it, as a hosted service or app for other
  people to sign up to, whether you charge for it or not.
- Publishing a fork that is marketed as a substitute for Alexandria.

Only the maker can do these, or someone the maker has said yes to in writing.
To ask, open an issue on GitHub titled "License request" and say what you want
to do.

The license also keeps this protection if the maker stops offering a hosted
version for a while (see "Licensor Line of Business" in the LICENSE file).

## "What if I rewrite it completely?"

The license covers Alexandria's code and things made from it. If you start
from this code, the license follows your changes, however large. If you build
your own app from scratch that does similar things, the license doesn't apply
to it: features, ideas and game rules can't be owned. There is no "changed
enough" line in between.

## Releases

Every release of Alexandria is under the PolyForm Shield license. There has
never been a release under another license.

## The name

"Alexandria" as the name of this app belongs to its maker. A fork or a rebuild
must use a different name and must not suggest it is the official Alexandria.

## Mods

Mods are meant to be free.

- **Your mod is yours.** A house rule or system you write is your own work, and
  you pick its license. It is not bound by the Shield license unless it copies
  Alexandria's code.
- **Built-in rules are MIT.** The house rules in `mods/` are under the MIT
  License in [mods/LICENSE](../mods/LICENSE), so you can copy them as a start
  for your own.
- **Sharing on Alexandria is free.** Any mod listed in Alexandria's mod catalog
  or shared through the repository's `mods/` folder must be free to download in
  full.
- **Donations are welcome.** A mod can link to Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, a tip
  jar or similar, and say thanks to people who give.
- **No paywalls.** No paid-only mods, paid early access, locked features or
  "pay to unlock" versions in Alexandria's catalog. Patreon or similar is fine
  as a way to donate, as long as everyone gets the same mod.

## Game content

Material from the System Reference Document 5.1 and 5.2, Open5e and other
sources keeps its own license (usually CC-BY-4.0) and its attribution. The
Shield license covers only Alexandria's own code.
